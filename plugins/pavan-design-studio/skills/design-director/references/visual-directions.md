# Visual Direction Library

Use these as starting vocabularies, not templates to copy.

- **Editorial precision** — strong typography, asymmetry, whitespace, restrained surfaces; good for portfolios, research, premium content.
- **Cinematic product** — large imagery/video, controlled darkness, dramatic scale, sparse copy; good for launches and experiential portfolios.
- **Industrial utility** — functional grids, dense information, clear borders, minimal decoration; good for developer and operations tools.
- **Precision SaaS** — clean hierarchy, quiet surfaces, highly legible forms/data, restrained accent use; good for serious B2B software.
- **Data cockpit** — dense but ordered information, strong grouping, comparison-first layouts; good for analytics and monitoring.
- **Neo-brutal** — strong geometry, explicit structure, intentional contrast and rawness; use selectively for expressive products.
- **Soft utility** — approachable spacing, calm type, gentle depth, warm illustration; good for consumer productivity and education.
- **Spatial layers** — foreground/background depth, anchored controls, progressive disclosure; good for complex interactive tools.
- **Developer terminal hybrid** — monospace used strategically with editorial typography, command-oriented interactions, precise status language.
- **Playful systems** — expressive illustration, motion, modular shapes, high feedback; good for games, creator and youth products.

For any direction define: 1 dominant idea, 1 type strategy, 1 spacing rhythm, 1 surface rule, 1 motion rule, and 3 things explicitly forbidden.
