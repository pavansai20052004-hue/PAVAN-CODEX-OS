# Motion Design

1. Give every animation a job: orient, explain causality, preserve continuity, signal state, or add deliberate character.
2. Prefer transform/opacity and short transitions for routine interactions.
3. Reserve cinematic motion for high-value moments; do not animate every surface.
4. Keep enter/exit direction consistent with spatial logic.
5. Support reduced-motion preferences and avoid motion that blocks input.
6. Test interrupted, repeated, fast-click, and navigation cases.
7. Measure performance when animation touches large lists, canvas, video, or heavy effects.
